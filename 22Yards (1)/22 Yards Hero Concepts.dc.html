Turn 3 · Campaign concepts · Hard & confident

Three campaigns, each with a device that survives cheap photography

Every one of these is built so a phone-shot or stock image can't sink it: the photograph is either contained in a plate, pushed to near-silhouette, or paired with an identically graded twin. The recognisable asset is the device — a timestamp, a seam, a location plate — not the picture. Social extensions sit under each hero.
